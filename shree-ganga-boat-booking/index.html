FAQ

Common questions

How do I confirm my booking?

Fill the form, choose payment method, and send the auto-generated WhatsApp request. Our team will confirm availability.

Are life jackets available?

Yes, life jackets are available for passengers on boat rides.

Can I book car and hotel services too?

Yes, rental car service, airport transfer and hotel booking can be requested from the same booking form.

What happens in bad weather?

For safety, rides may be delayed or rescheduled when river or weather conditions are not suitable.

What is the best time for a sunrise boat ride?

The sunrise boat ride starts at 5:30 AM. Early morning gives calmer waters, softer light and peaceful views of the ghats before the crowds arrive.

Do you offer pickup from the airport or railway station?

Yes, we offer airport and railway station pickup services. You can book a cab or request a combined travel + hotel package.
